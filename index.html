Methods

Network. GTFS supplies scheduled transit trips; OSM PBF supplies streets and modal access. Coordinates connect to an eligible street vertex within 150 m. Short straight connectors approximate access without checking barriers.

Prism. Forward earliest-arrival and backward latest-departure searches identify feasible network travel between ordered anchors. Each anchor enforces its exact start and full stay. A finite walking limit is shared across access, transfers and egress, using time-and-walking labels; a blank limit is unlimited.

Travel time. Walking and biking use the specified speeds in m/s. Driving uses OSM speed limits or road-class defaults multiplied by the driving factor. Transit uses the selected GTFS service day, walking connections and boarding buffer. Waiting is allowed at network vertices.

Views. Bands show feasible travel times along network links. The time slice shows feasible positions at the selected time. GTFS shapes interpolate bus positions by distance between timed stops. Click a prism feature to select its time; drag to rotate.

Scope. No real-time delays, traffic, signals, turn restrictions, elevation, node barriers, parking or GTFS-specific transfer rules. Walking may follow road centerlines. Times above 24:00 are supported; frequency-based and blank-time feeds are rejected. Coverage is limited by the supplied extract. Large 3D views simplify displayed streets and bands; time slices and data export retain all feasible links.
